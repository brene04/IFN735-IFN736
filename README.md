# Competency-Based Human Resource Management System

## IFN735 / IFN736 Capstone Project

A work-integrated learning project between QUT's Team Innov8 and the Philippines Department of Science and Technology (DOST) to develop a centralised **Competency-Based Human Resource Management System (CBHRMS)**.

The system aims to support competency profiling, skills-gap analysis, workforce analytics, and related HR processes through an integrated web-based platform.

> **Project Status:** Phase 1 prototype in development  
> **Semester:** Semester 2, 2026

---

## Project Overview

The Competency-Based Human Resource Management System is being developed to provide a centralised platform for managing and analysing employee competencies and workforce information.

The project focuses on creating an integrated system that can support:

- Employee competency profiling
- Competency and skills-gap analysis
- Workforce and HR analytics
- Competency-based workforce planning
- Centralised management of HR-related information
- Reporting and decision support
- Data-driven workforce management

The system is being developed iteratively in consultation with the DOST client and will evolve based on validated requirements, feedback, and prototype evaluations.
